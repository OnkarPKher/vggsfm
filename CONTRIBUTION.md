## Original Project Summary

- **Date:** Nov 2024 – Dec 2024
- **Tools:** PyTorch, NeRF, VGG-SfM, LLFF Dataset, synthetic Lego, PSNR evaluation

## My Specific Contributions
- Implemented NeRF benchmarking pipeline on the LLFF and Lego datasets
- Applied hierarchical sampling and positional encoding
- Evaluated visual quality using PSNR (achieved ~30 dB)
- Reconstructed sparse 3D points from VGG-SfM using differentiable triangulation
- Compared generalization on custom input images
